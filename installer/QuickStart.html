Stilleto

Your voice. Anywhere you type.

Stilleto turns your speech into text in any Windows application: Notepad, Word, browsers, email, chat apps and code editors. Speech recognition runs entirely on this PC; no account or internet connection is needed.

Dictate in four steps

  1. Click where you want the text to go, in any application.
  2. Press Ctrl + Alt + Space. A small gold widget appears near the bottom of the screen: Stilleto is listening.
  3. Speak naturally. Fillers ("um", "uh") and stammered starts ("I-I-I") are removed automatically.
  4. Press Ctrl + Alt + Space again. After a moment the text appears at your cursor.

Stilleto keeps running in the system tray (bottom-right, near the clock) when you close its window, so the shortcut always works. Right-click the tray icon to start or stop dictation, pause the shortcut, open Settings or quit.

First launch

  • The recommended speech model (Base, English) is installed with Stilleto, so you can dictate straight away.
  • If Windows asks, allow microphone access. You can check it under Windows Settings > Privacy & security > Microphone ("Let desktop apps access your microphone" must be on).
  • Windows SmartScreen may warn about an unrecognised app because this test build is not code-signed. Choose More info > Run anyway.

Useful settings

Change the shortcutSettings > Keyboard shortcut > Change
Choose a microphoneSettings > Audio. Use Test microphone to check the level.
Other languagesModels > download a Multilingual model, then Settings > Transcription > Language.
Better accuracyModels > Small (English). More accurate for names and jargon, slower on older PCs.
Keep fillers as spokenSettings > Text insertion > Remove fillers and stutters
Apps that block pasteSettings > Text insertion > Method > Typing
Start with WindowsSettings > General > Launch at Windows sign-in
History and privacySettings > Privacy (turn history off, clear transcripts)

Good to know

  • Your clipboard is used briefly to paste the text and is then put back.
  • If you switch to another window before the text is ready, Stilleto does not type it anywhere. Open Stilleto and use Insert or Copy on the Dictation or History page.
  • Windows does not allow typing into programs running as administrator, password fields or the UAC prompt.
  • Transcription starts when you stop recording; live word-by-word typing is not available yet.

Troubleshooting

Nothing happens on the shortcutAnother app may already use Ctrl+Alt+Space. Stilleto shows this on the Overview page; choose another shortcut in Settings.
"No speech detected"Check the microphone in Settings > Audio > Test microphone, and Windows' microphone privacy setting.
Text not insertedThe target may run as administrator, or focus changed. The transcript is kept in Stilleto to copy or insert.
"Cannot run on this PC"Stilleto needs a processor with AVX2 (Intel 4th generation, AMD Ryzen or newer).

Reporting a problem

Open Help & About > Open log and include stilleto.log with a short description of what you did. The log records events and the target program's name, never your dictated text.

Where things are stored

Settings, history, log%APPDATA%\Stilleto
Downloaded models%LOCALAPPDATA%\Stilleto\models
Installed programC:\Program Files\Stilleto

Uninstall from Windows Settings > Apps or the Start Menu. Your settings and history stay in %APPDATA%\Stilleto, and downloaded models in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Stilleto\models; delete those folders to remove them too.
